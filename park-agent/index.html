Park Agent

Find a spot. Free one up. Everyone parks faster.

A mobile app and API, built with Expo / React Native and ASP.NET Core
The problem

Drivers circle for parking while empty spots go unannounced

1

Nobody knows what is free

Public parking data is thin, and often a proxy such as "the meter is in service", not "the spot is empty".

2

The person who knows stays quiet

A driver walking back to their car knows a spot is about to open, but has no way to tell the people nearby.

3

No reason to share

Even a helpful driver gets nothing for it, so almost nobody does it.

The idea

Give to get: sharing a spot pays, taking one costs a little

Drivers who are about to leave announce their spot. Drivers looking for one unlock it. Credits and reputation keep it fair.

Finding a spot

Community spots come first

  • Spots other drivers are leaving sit at the top, then everything else, nearest first.
  • Locked shows the driver's first name and an approximate area.
  • ★ Verified means the driver has already left, so the spot is probably free now.
  • Public spots carry a chip for their source: garage, sensor or municipal.
A starred spot above a locked one, then regular spots.
Community spots

Private by design

A community spot is another person, so the app reveals as little as it can until you commit.

The blur is the same every time, so repeated searches cannot average it away. Other drivers never see an email address.

Leaving parking

A verified departure keeps the spot on offer

GPS verification pays a reduced reward once, and a departed spot is never penalised as a no-show. The leaver can announce again straight away.

Fair play

Four rules keep the economy from being farmed

Daily cap

At most 5 rewarded confirmations per account per day. Beyond that the spot still works, but pays nothing.

Minimum time before "I've left"

It counts only 2 minutes after announcing, so walking off straight away proves nothing.

Be at the spot to say "I parked"

The seeker's position is checked: within 75 m of a community spot.

Repeated pairs are not paid

The same two accounts trading spots stop earning after 2 trades in 24 hours, and it is logged.

A driver who really leaves or parks is never blocked, only unpaid past the limits. All the numbers are server settings.

Under the hood

One API owns the rules and the data

Both sides are built against one shared contract file. A background job every 30 seconds ends no-shows and releases lapsed claims.
